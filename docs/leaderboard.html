mAP@0.5 (ranking metric): a detection is correct when its box overlaps a real sign of the same class by at least 50% (IoU), averaged over the 4 sign classes; 1.0 is perfect. mAP@[0.5:0.95]: the same, averaged over stricter overlaps from 50% to 95% (COCO standard), so boxes must fit tightly.
